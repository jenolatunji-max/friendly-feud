Your first game in 5 steps

  1. Use a laptop or desktopWindows, Mac or Chromebook, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Connect your TVPlug your laptop into the TV with an HDMI cable, or cast/AirPlay the screen. Set the display to Extend (Windows: Windows+P. Mac: System Settings, Displays, turn off mirroring).
  3. Click Quick PlayPick a question pack and click Start game. A second window opens: that's the TV board.
  4. Drag the TV board onto your TVClick it and press F for full screen. Your laptop keeps the answers. The room only sees the board.
  5. Play!Click an answer to flip it, press X for a strike, and U to undo. The "Say" lines tell you what to say next.

Good to know

  • Your Crowd: text your questions to guests before the party, paste their replies back in, and the board fills with their real answers.
  • One screen? Choose One screen in Custom Game. Hold H for the host panel, and print host cards.
  • Sound: click Sound on at the top of the host screen (or press M) to mute. Turn off single sounds in Settings.
  • Buzzers: USB buzzers that type a key work. Set them in Settings.
  • Zoom, Teams or Meet: share just the TV board window, with "share sound" on.
  • Your own questions: Home, Edit Questions. Duplicate any pack to change it.
  • Saved automatically: your packs and surveys stay in the browser you use. Use Settings, Export backup to move them.

Troubleshooting

The TV window didn't open.
Your browser blocked the pop-up. Click the blocked pop-up icon in the address bar, choose Always allow, then try again.
The TV shows the same as my laptop.
Switch your display to Extend instead of Mirror (see step 2).
No sound from the TV.
Pick the TV as your sound output, and check the game isn't muted.
The page refreshed mid-game.
Open it again and choose Pick up where you left off.
The download opened in the wrong app.
Unzip it first. Then right-click index.html, choose Open with, and pick your browser.
Can I use an iPad or phone?
Please use a laptop or desktop computer.
