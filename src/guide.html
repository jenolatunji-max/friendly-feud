Quick-Start Guide

The survey game show for your living room. No PowerPoint. No macros. No sign up. No internet needed.

Game night, step by step

  1. DownloadDownload the zip file from your Etsy purchase page (Purchases and reviews) on a laptop or desktop computer.
  2. UnzipWindows: right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Mac: double-click the zip. Chromebook: open it in Files and copy the files out.
  3. Double-click index.htmlIt opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Nothing to install, enable or unblock.
  4. Open the TV boardPlug your laptop into your TV with an HDMI cable, or cast/AirPlay the screen. Set the display to Extend. Click Quick Play, drag the TV board window onto the TV, click it and press F.
  5. Play!Your laptop shows the answers. The TV shows the board. Click an answer to flip it, press X for a strike and U to undo.
  6. Before guests arriveRun Setup Check on the home screen. It tests your TV, sound and buzzers in about a minute.

Good to know

  • Your Crowd: text your questions to guests, paste their replies back in, and the board fills with their real answers.
  • One screen? Choose One screen in Custom Game. Hold H for the host panel, and print host cards.
  • Sounds: Settings, Sound. Turn off any sound, or mute all.
  • Buzzers: USB buzzers that type a key work. Set them in Settings.
  • Keys: 1-8 reveal, X strike, U undo, N next round, F full screen.
  • Needs: a laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac or Chromebook). Not for iPad or phone.

Troubleshooting

The TV window didn't open.
Allow pop-ups: click the blocked pop-up icon in the address bar, choose Always allow.
The TV shows the same thing as my laptop.
Switch the display to Extend (Windows: Win+P. Mac: Displays, turn off mirroring).
No sound from the TV.
Pick the TV as your sound output, and check Settings, Sound is not muted.
The page refreshed mid-game.
Open it again and choose Pick up where you left off.
It opened in the wrong app.
Right-click index.html, choose Open with, and pick your browser.
Can I edit the questions?
Yes. Home, Edit Questions. Duplicate any pack to change it.
