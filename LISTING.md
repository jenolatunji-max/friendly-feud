## Description

**No macros to enable. No PowerPoint. Nothing to install.**

Friendly Feud is a survey-style game show for your living room. Two teams face off to guess the most popular answers. Download it, double-click one file, and it opens in your web browser. Plug your laptop into your TV and you're hosting a real game show in minutes.

It works with no internet connection, on a work laptop, at Grandma's house, anywhere.

**Everything included. No add-ons to buy.**

- The Lightning Round bonus round is built in, not sold separately.
- No PowerPoint, no macros, no "enable content" warnings. Nothing can stop working halfway through your party.
- Tiny download (under 200 KB). Opens in seconds, even on slow Wi-Fi.
- Turn the sound off in one click, or switch off just the "ding".
- Made a mistake? Undo it. Page closed? Your game comes right back.

**What's included**

- Friendly Feud game file (works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari)

**FAQ**

**Do I need PowerPoint or Google Slides?**
No. It runs in your web browser. There is nothing to install and no macros to enable.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. Everything is inside one file. It works offline.

**Will it work on my work laptop?**
Yes, if you can open a web page, you can play. It is a plain file that opens in your browser.

**Can I add my own questions?**
Yes. Edit any pack, write your own, or build a board from your guests' answers with Your Crowd.

**Can I play on one screen?**
Yes. One-screen mode shows the board full screen, and you hold H to see the host panel. You can also print host cards.

**Are the answers from a real survey?**
The built-in packs are our best estimates of the most popular answers from American adults. For real survey answers, use Your Crowd to survey your own guests.

**Can I play over Zoom?**
Yes. Share the TV board window in Zoom, Teams or Google Meet, with "share sound" turned on.

**Will we run out of questions?**
There are 205 built-in questions, and the game plays the ones you haven't seen first. You can add as many of your own as you like.

**Can I use it on an iPad or phone?**
Not right now. Please use a laptop or desktop computer.

**Is this the TV show?**
No. Friendly Feud is an independent party game. It is not affiliated with any TV show.

This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
